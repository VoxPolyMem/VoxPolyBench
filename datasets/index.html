01 / THE BENCHMARK

Memory is social.
Speech makes it harder.

Each scenario follows a recurring group through 8 to 12 sessions. A system must connect voices and participants over time, track changing information, and answer questions grounded in the shared history.

01
◌

Recurring speakers

Participants return across sessions with stable voices, creating an identity thread through the history.

02
↗

Evolving memory

Plans, preferences, and facts are updated, contradicted, and resolved over multiple conversations.

03
↔

Directed interaction

Questions may depend on the source speaker, the addressee, or who should receive a response.

04
◎

Personalized answers

The same shared history can support different answers for different participants.
